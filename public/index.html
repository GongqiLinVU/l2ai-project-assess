What is excluded?

Maximum 5 files, 30 KB each and 90 KB combined. Text allowlist only. Hidden paths, likely secrets, dependencies, lockfiles, build output, binary files and symlinks are excluded. Content is checked for common credential patterns before analysis. These checks cannot guarantee detection of every secret; review public materials before selecting them.
